Managing DirectorProject Direction & FID-Readiness

Dirk Bodenheim

Professional background

Dirk Bodenheim, born in 1965, studied mechanical engineering and completed an MBA as an additional qualification. Later, alongside his professional career, he obtained certification as a Project Management Professional (PMP) through the Project Management Institute (PMI).

His career began in 1990 at what was then Siemens KWU (today Siemens Energy), working in the commissioning of conventional power plants. After several international construction sites and commissioning assignments, he moved to Singapore in 1994 to join De Nora as Head of Engineering, later becoming Technical Director. In 1998, he joined thyssenkrupp Uhde as Project Manager for electrolysis plants (chlor-alkali technology, which was later further developed for water electrolysis to produce green hydrogen). As part of internal restructuring, he moved into central Project Management, where he executed numerous EPC lump-sum turnkey megaprojects across various technologies, including gasification and ammonia. From 2021 to 2024, he headed the Project Management division. In this role, he led an internal initiative that defined the modularization of "green" ammonia plants. 2025 Dirk Bodenheim founded FELcilitate consulting GmbH where he is Managing Director until today.

  • ›Team building and kick-off workshops

  • ›Planning, initiation/ramp-up ("the first 100 days"), and closeout of projects
  • ›Contract analysis, risk management, schedule and performance management (KPIs), project controls
  • ›Site and commissioning planning and execution, completion management
  • ›Modularization, AWP (Advanced Work Packaging)
Managing DirectorProcess Engineering & Technology

Ludger Schlüter

Professional background

Ludger Schlüter is a graduate process engineer with more than 30 years in international large-scale plant engineering, having moved from process and mechanical engineering into project management, engineering leadership and general management. He studied process engineering in a dual programme with BASF at the Baden-Württemberg Cooperative State University in Mannheim and later earned a further degree in process engineering and thermodynamics at the University of Stuttgart alongside his work. He is a certified International Welding Engineer (IWE) and, since 2024, a certified ESG Implementation Manager.

His career began in 1992 at BASF in Ludwigshafen, where over nine years he worked as process engineer, engineering manager and project manager on investment projects in Germany, Belgium, Korea and the USA, including an assignment in Louisiana for the engineering and technology transfer of an agro active-ingredient plant. In 2001 he joined Lurgi, later Air Liquide, in Frankfurt, leading its static-equipment and mechanical departments, before moving to Beijing in 2009 as Managing Director for China, where he reorganised the joint venture and grew order intake from zero to over €80 million within two years. From 2011 to 2015 he held director positions at Alstom Thermal Power — first in Beijing, building the technology, engineering and project-management organisation for CO₂ capture and storage in Asia, then in Paris as Director Industrialisation for Nuclear Products, restructuring the global nuclear thermal business across France, Switzerland and China.

In 2015 he joined thyssenkrupp Industrial Solutions (Uhde) in Dortmund, leading the Balance of Plant & Mechanical department and later the global discipline, responsible for mechanical equipment and infrastructure in large EPC lump-sum turnkey projects. From 2018 to 2023 he was Senior Vice President, Global Corporate Engineering at Bayer in Leverkusen, heading an organisation of some 200 engineers and an annual capital portfolio of around €1 billion, and driving restructuring, digitalisation (BIM) and modularization. In 2023 he founded EUSTRATEGO GmbH & Co. KG, an interim-management and consulting firm which he leads as Managing Director. In interim and advisory mandates he has served as Chief Operating Officer of a hydrogen project developer and producer, led the task force that resolved the hydrogen-compressor interfaces of one of the world’s largest green-hydrogen plants in Sweden, managed the FEED of a 100 MW fuel-cell power plant for a data centre, and advised on contractor selection for an LNG terminal.

Practical experience and special expertise
  • ›Over 30 years in international large-scale plant engineering and EPC projects (chemicals, oil & gas, energy, pharma and agro)
  • ›General management and P&L responsibility (Managing Director, COO), including business turnaround
  • ›Building, leading and developing global engineering organisations and matrix teams across Europe and Asia
  • ›Large cross-functional investment projects from development and engineering through to commissioning (portfolios up to €4bn, single projects up to €3bn)
  • ›Transformation, change management, restructuring and cost reduction of 25–30 %
  • ›Damage, claim and crisis management, including negotiations with clients, suppliers and insurers
  • ›Mechanical and static-equipment engineering, compressors, utilities and steam systems
  • ›Digitalisation (BIM) and plant modularization
  • ›CO₂ capture and storage (CCS), hydrogen and energy investment projects
  • ›ESG and EU-taxonomy implementation
Managing DirectorLegal, Contracts & Governance

Marc Ackermann

Professional background

Marc Ackermann is a hands-on corporate lawyer and trusted business partner who combines the proficiency of a fully qualified German attorney with the mindset of an entrepreneur. After working as a banker at Deutsche Bank, he studied law at the Ruhr-University Bochum, with a specialised year at the Centre for Energy, Petroleum and Mineral Law & Policy at the University of Dundee (UK).

His career began in 2002 in private practice as an attorney for private and public construction law before he moved in-house at ThyssenKrupp Uhde GmbH. From 2013 he served as Head of Legal (global responsibility) at thyssenkrupp Industrial Solutions AG, structuring and negotiating international EPC and cooperation contracts of large-scale chemical plant engineering projects. He then took on successive General Counsel mandates: at SEG Automotive Germany GmbH, where as General Counsel and Chief Compliance Officer he built the legal and compliance function of a global automotive supplier following its carve-out from Bosch; at Compleo Charging Solutions AG in the eMobility sector; and at Hynamics Deutschland GmbH (EDF Group), where as General Counsel and member of the management team he co-steered the strategy and structuring of green-hydrogen energy investment projects. In 2020 he founded NAMREKA GmbH in Dortmund, which he leads as Managing Director and General Counsel.

Across more than two decades in international industry, Marc has consistently built legal and compliance functions from the ground up and translated complex legal questions into clear, commercially sound decisions. He works close to the business, at eye level with management, engineers and clients alike, guided by entrepreneurial thinking, technical understanding and people leadership with both heart and mind. Colleagues and clients regularly sum up the experience of working with him in a single sentence: “I simply get better with you at my side.” It is this combination of legal excellence and a genuine consultant-of-trust attitude that defines the way he works.

  • ›Drafting, reviewing and negotiating complex project contracts (EPC, PPA, supply, engineering, consortium/JV, licence, IT; model contracts such as FIDIC and NEC)

Cost, Performance & AI Integration

Dustin Mayor

Professional background

Dustin Mayor, studied mechanical engineering and completed an MBA at WHU – Otto Beisheim School of Management, with international modules at Columbia Business School, Fudan University and the Indian Institute of Management. He is a Certified Estimating Professional (CEP®) according to AACE® and a certified user of the Aspen Capital Cost Estimator (ACCE).

His career began in 2008 at Alstom Power Systems with a dual study program and assignments in Germany, China and Italy. In 2011, he joined thyssenkrupp, where he spent 15 years in industrial plant engineering: equipment engineering and site supervision for ammonia plants in the Middle East and North Africa, followed by group strategy and PMO roles – for several years as thyssenkrupp’s project lead for internal strategy, transformation, organization and restructuring projects delivered with consultancies such as McKinsey, PwC, Bain and others, including the Board Office with direct reporting to the CEO. In 2022, he moved to Thailand to further strengthen the modularization business, before leading the global bid estimation initiative “Workflow and Tools” at thyssenkrupp Uhde. Today he advises owners, contractors and investors on CAPEX projects: e.g. cost estimation, Cold Eye Reviews, schedule analysis and PDRI – increasingly AI-supported.

  • ›Focus on plant modularization and logistics concepts
  • ›Project lead for internal strategy, transformation and restructuring programs with leading consultancies (McKinsey, PwC, Bain, etc.)
  • ›Leading Board Office with reporting to the CEO

Ulrich Barow

Professional background

Ulrich Barow, born in 1964, studied civil engineering after his 2 year military service. His career began in 1984 when he started working at a construction site before studying and continued during studies at various sites of his first employer STRABAG. In 1995 he changed to WALTER BAU for new challenging projects (Metro tunnel in Berlin, pumped hydro storage plant in Thuringia, Metro tunnel in Algeria). From 2006 on he led the power plant business unit of HOCHTIEF with projects in Germany (waste, coal, geothermal), United Kingdom (waste) and Finland (nuclear). In 2013, he joined thyssenkrupp Uhde as Head of Global Construction, later also responsible for the construction of cement plants and the mining business of thyssenkrupp Industrial Solutions (Uhde, Polysius & Mining). From mid 2014 to spring 2016, he headed in parallel to his line function a CAN (Calcium Ammonium Nitrate) plant in Turkey as a Project Director and later as Operations Manager. In summer 2019 he decided to leave thyssenkrupp and to start his own consulting engineering business (refinery, chemical plants, civil infrastructure). In 2025/26 he was reactivated by thyssenkrupp Uhde for a program in east Algeria.
